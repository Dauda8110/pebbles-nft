The Philosophy

An homage to simplicity, humor, and digital craft.

Deep in the quiet streams and gentle shores, small stones have developed rather large personalities. **Pebbles** is a fine-art collection of 6,666 unique, digitally-curated realistic stones.

By placing expressive plastic googly eyes and playful hand-inked mouths onto high-definition photography of organic stones, we've created a digital gallery that is whimsically simple yet exceptionally premium. No cyberpunk darkness. No bloated web3 clutter. Just warm, tactile joy on cheerful solid backdrops.
